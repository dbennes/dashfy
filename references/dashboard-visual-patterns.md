## Lessons To Reuse

- Build dashboards from compact cards, not large empty panels.
- Use one primary score card per section with denominator visible.
- Prefer separated chart cards for each management question.
- Use horizontal progress bars and ranking bars for operational comparison.
- Use KPI strips for quick scanning: label, number, denominator/delta.
- Use small multiples when several metrics share equal importance.
- Use muted surfaces and subtle borders; let data colors carry meaning.
- Keep tables below summary charts for drill-down and auditability.
- Vary layout by module while keeping consistent density, card styling, typography, and spacing.

## Engineering Application

- Main percentage is AFC / total documents.
- In this eClic view, AFC is represented by revision C.
- Engineering charts should include:
  - AFC score card with progress rail and count/total.
  - Status documental distribution as a stacked/composition rail.
  - Revision maturity as a management funnel: AFC score, revision composition strip, and relative stage rails with explicit percentages.
  - Discipline concentration as lollipop/ranking bars.
